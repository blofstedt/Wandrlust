# Safety Disclaimer

**Version 1.0**

## Wandrlust is a tool, not a guardian angel.

This app exists to **augment** your overlanding. It is not a safety service and
it cannot keep you safe.

- It cannot summon help.
- It does not monitor you.
- If nobody hears from you, it will not notice.

## Boundaries are approximate

Every boundary shown is an agency *management* area, not a surveyed property
line. Government sources state plainly that their data does not depict
ownership. This is why edges are drawn as a fade rather than a line.

**Near any boundary, assume you may be on private land.** Private inholdings
inside public land are frequently not shown at all.

## Alerts can fail

Weather, fire and flood alerts come from the National Weather Service and
Environment Canada. We pass them along; we do not issue or verify them.

Delivery fails for reasons entirely outside our control: no signal, phone off,
dead battery, rotated push subscription, service outage, revoked permission.
Weather also moves faster than any alert system.

**Carry a satellite communicator or emergency beacon** if a missed alert would
matter where you are going.

## Fire restrictions

Fire bans are legal restrictions issued by individual land agencies, and they
change frequently. A Red Flag Warning is a *forecast*; a Stage 2 restriction is
a *prohibition*. Always confirm current fire rules with the managing agency
before lighting anything.

## Posted signage wins

If a gate is closed, or a sign says no camping, that is authoritative. This app
is not.

## Before you go

- Bring paper maps
- Bring more water than you think you need
- Tell someone your route and expected return
- Carry a way to call for help that does not depend on cell coverage
- Check conditions with the agencies whose job it is to know them

**Use the app to make your trip better — not to make it safe.**
