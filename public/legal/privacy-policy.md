# Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0**

## The short version

We store your name, username, email address, password, and location. We use
that to run the app. **We never sell your data, and we never share it with
third-party vendors.** It lives in a secure database.

That's the whole policy in four sentences. The detail below exists because
specifics matter, not because the summary is hiding anything.

---

## What we collect

**Account information**

- First and last name
- Username
- Email address
- Password

Your password is never stored as text you or we could read. It is hashed by
our authentication provider, which means even we cannot see it. If you sign in
with Google, we never receive a password at all.

**Location**

We collect location data to make the app work: showing campsites near you,
telling you which land rules apply where you are standing, and sending alerts
for fires, floods and storms in your area.

Two things worth knowing about how we handle it:

- **Shared location is deliberately imprecise.** If you turn on camper
  presence, other users see your position rounded to roughly one kilometre.
  Your exact coordinates never leave your device.
- **We don't keep a location history.** Presence records expire after four
  hours and are deleted. There is no table anywhere recording where you have
  been.

**Content you create**

Campsite submissions, reviews, photos, check-ins, hazard reports and property
listings. This is the shared knowledge that makes the app useful, so it's
visible to other users.

**Technical information**

Browser type and device information, used to deliver notifications correctly
and to diagnose problems.

---

## What we do with it

We use your information to:

- Sign you in and keep your account secure
- Show you campsites, land boundaries and local rules where you are
- Send weather, fire, flood and storm alerts you have asked for
- Run the token, trust and hosting features
- Fix bugs and keep the service running

That's it. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not analyse your
movements for any purpose beyond delivering the features above.

---

## What we never do

**We never sell your data.** Not to advertisers, data brokers, marketers,
analytics companies, or anyone else. There is no circumstance in which your
information is a product we offer.

**We never share it with third-party vendors** for their own purposes.

The only parties who ever touch your data are the infrastructure providers who
host it on our behalf — the database, authentication and notification services
the app runs on. They process it under contract, solely to provide that
service, and cannot use it for anything else.

We may disclose information if legally compelled to by valid legal process. If
that happens and we are permitted to tell you, we will.

---

## Where it lives

Your data is stored in a secure, access-controlled database. Every table
enforces row-level security, which means the database itself refuses to return
another user's private data even if the application asks for it incorrectly.

Passwords are hashed. Connections are encrypted in transit.

---

## Your choices

- **Location sharing is off by default.** Camper presence starts in Ghost
  mode. You opt in, per session, and can turn it off at any time.
- **Alerts are opt-in.** You choose which categories you receive, and the
  radius.
- **Road data contribution is optional.** Scout Mode only runs when you start
  it.

## Your rights

You can:

- **See your data** — everything tied to your account
- **Correct it** — edit your profile at any time
- **Delete it** — deleting your account removes your personal information.
  Content you contributed to shared knowledge, like campsite conditions, may
  remain in anonymised form so the map doesn't degrade for everyone else.
- **Take it with you** — request an export in a portable format

To exercise any of these, contact us at the address below.

---

## Children

Wandrlust is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, tell
us and we will remove it.

---

## Changes

If we change this policy meaningfully, we will tell you in the app and ask you
to review it. We keep a record of which version you agreed to, so "you accepted
the terms" always refers to a specific document rather than a moving target.
