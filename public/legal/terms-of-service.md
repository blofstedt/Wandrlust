## 1. What Wandrlust is

A trip-planning aid for dispersed camping and overlanding. It shows approximate
public land boundaries, community-reported campsites, weather and hazard alerts,
and lets campers share information with each other.

Wandrlust does not drive you anywhere. When you ask for directions it hands the
coordinates to your phone's own maps app — Apple Maps or Google Maps — and
everything from that point on is theirs, including whatever they show on
CarPlay or Android Auto. Those apps do not know what Wandrlust knows about the
last few miles: whether the road it routed you down is maintained, passable in
your rig, or a road at all.

## 2. What Wandrlust is not

- **Not a safety or emergency service.** It will not summon help. It does not
  monitor you. If nobody hears from you, the app will not notice.
- **Not an authority on land ownership.** Boundaries shown are approximate
  management areas, not surveyed property lines.
- **Not a substitute for official sources.** Land agencies, weather services
  and emergency management authorities are authoritative. We are not.
- **Not a guarantee that camping is legal anywhere shown.**

## 5. Boundary data is approximate

This deserves its own section because it is the most likely way the app could
mislead you.

**No boundary shown is survey-grade.** Property lines are established by
licensed surveyors and land title records, not by any map in any app. Our
sources are government agency management boundaries, which explicitly state
they do not depict ownership.

That is why edges are drawn as a **fade rather than a line**. Inside that band,
you may be on either side of the real boundary.

Private inholdings inside public land are frequently not shown at all.

**Near any boundary, assume you may be on private land.** If it matters, verify
with the county recorder, the land title registry, or the managing agency
before you park.

## 6. Weather and hazard alerts

Alerts come from the National Weather Service and Environment Canada. We pass
them along; we do not issue them and we do not verify them.

**Never rely on this app to warn you.** Notifications fail for reasons entirely
outside our control: no signal, phone off, battery dead, browser rotated your
subscription, push service outage, permission revoked. Weather can also move
faster than any alert system.

Fire restrictions in particular change frequently and are issued by individual
land agencies. Always confirm current fire rules with the managing agency
before lighting anything.

Carry a satellite communicator or emergency beacon if you are travelling
somewhere a failed notification would matter.

## 7. Land access and legality

You are responsible for confirming that camping is legal where you are.

Rules vary by agency, by unit, and by season, and change with closures, fire
restrictions and emergency orders — most of which exist as posted signage and
PDFs, not as data any app can read.

**Follow posted signage over anything shown in this app.** If a gate is closed
or a sign says no camping, that is authoritative and we are not.

Respect Leave No Trace principles, stay limits, and the land you are on.
